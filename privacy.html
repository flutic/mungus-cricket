Mungus Cricket

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Mungus Cricket collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and the app makes no network connections of its own. Everything you make in it is kept on your device, or in your own iCloud Drive if you use it.

What the app collects

Nothing. The developer does not receive, store or see anything you do in Mungus Cricket: not your leagues, players, matches or settings, and not any information about you or your device.

  • No sign-in or account of any kind.
  • No analytics, usage measurement or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No advertising.
  • No third-party code that collects data.

Where your data is kept

Mungus Cricket keeps each league (a data set) as a folder of ordinary files.

  • On your device. The Runcible Cricket League and any New World you start are kept in the app’s own storage on your iPhone or iPad. You can see the folder in the Files app, under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Mungus Cricket. Like the rest of your device, it is included in your device backups.
  • In your iCloud Drive. When iCloud Drive is turned on for Mungus Cricket, an Empty World you start is kept in the Mungus Cricket folder of your iCloud Drive, and any data set you put in that folder is listed in the app. iCloud Drive is Apple’s service, tied to your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it.

Nothing leaves your device unless you send it yourself, for example by sharing a scorecard PDF or copying a league’s folder out of the app.

Widgets

The Mungus Cricket widgets show results from your own leagues. They read a small summary that the app writes into storage it shares with its widgets on your device. That summary never leaves the device.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports. That is Apple’s system: you can turn it on or off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. Mungus Cricket does not add anything to it.

Children

Mungus Cricket is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mungus Cricket changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
