Mungus Cricket

Support

Mungus Cricket is a stats-based cricket simulation for iPhone and iPad. Multi-day matches are played ball by ball from each player’s ratings, with the weather, the pitch, the light and the clock all taking their part. You captain both sides: pick the XIs, set the field’s aggression and the batters’ tempo, change the bowling, declare. Everything is recorded: full scorecards, league tables, averages, career cards, wagon wheels and run charts, and a scorer you can ask about the match as it happens.

It needs no account and no internet connection.

Getting started

The first time you open Mungus Cricket it offers three ways in.

Play the Runcible Cricket League

A league already part-way through its season, with six teams, their squads and grounds, the matches played so far and the fixtures still to come. One tap and you are setting up the next match. This is the quickest way to see what the app does.

Start a New World

A league of your own in four steps: choose how many teams and how long the matches are, name the teams, and the app rolls a ground and a squad of players for each, with a season of fixtures to play. Every name can be changed.

Start an Empty World

A blank league for building by hand: grounds, teams, players, squads, competitions and fixtures. The Set Up checklist, under Maintenance › Settings, shows what a match still needs.

To play a match, open the Match tab and follow the steps, or tap the next fixture. To start another league later, choose Switch World (at the top of the Maintenance tab on an iPhone, in the sidebar’s Maintenance section on an iPad) and pick one of the ways in under Start Something New.

Questions

Where is my data kept?

Each league (a world) is a folder of ordinary files. The Runcible Cricket League and a New World are kept on your device: in the Files app they are under On My iPhone › Mungus Cricket › datasets (On My iPad on an iPad). When iCloud Drive is turned on for Mungus Cricket, an Empty World is kept in iCloud Drive › Mungus Cricket. In the Worlds list, a folder icon means on this device and a cloud icon means iCloud Drive.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up a league?

Leagues on your device are included in your iPhone or iPad backups, to iCloud or to a computer. For a copy of your own, open the Files app, go to On My iPhone › Mungus Cricket › datasets, and copy the league’s folder to iCloud Drive or anywhere else. Quit Mungus Cricket first, so that no match is being saved while you copy.

You can also export one competition as a new world, with Export Competition as a World… in its menu in the Browse tab. The new world sits beside the old one, and its folder can be copied like any other.

How do I delete a league?

Choose Switch World, swipe left on the world and tap Delete. This cannot be undone.

What is the Runcible Cricket League?

The sample league that comes with the app: a fictional competition from 1986 with its own teams, players, grounds and results, part-way through its season. Each time you add it you get a fresh copy that is yours to play, edit or delete. The copy in the app is never changed, so you can always start it again: choose Switch World, then Play the Runcible Cricket League under Start Something New.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet. This version is for iPhone and iPad.
